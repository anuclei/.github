# Anuclei

**Building intelligent systems that operate at the speed of decision.**

We design and ship AI-native infrastructure, autonomous agents, and orchestration engines purpose-built for production environments. Our work sits at the intersection of large language models, distributed systems, and cloud-native architecture.

---

### What We Do

- **Autonomous Agent Systems** -- Multi-agent architectures that reason, plan, and execute across complex workflows without human-in-the-loop bottlenecks.
- **AI Orchestration at Scale** -- Scalable engines for coordinating multi-modal workloads across distributed infrastructure with minimal latency and resource overhead.
- **Cloud-Native AI Infrastructure** -- Kubernetes-native platforms that bring AI capabilities directly into the compute fabric where your applications already run.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation** -- Production-grade RAG pipelines that ground model outputs in your data, delivering accurate and context-aware responses.

---

### Engineering Principles

We build with a bias toward systems that are **observable**, **resilient**, and **operationally simple**. Every component we ship is designed to run unsupervised in production, degrade gracefully under pressure, and expose the telemetry needed to understand its behavior at any point in time.

Our stack is rooted in **Go**, **Kubernetes**, and the broader cloud-native ecosystem. We treat infrastructure as a first-class product, not an afterthought.
